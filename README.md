## I'm Kinda Likin' the Vibe: Tracking Sentiment Shifts in a Filipino AI App-Building Community


### Subject

Since the emergence of AI-assisted tools, the barrier to entry for creating applications has been lowered significantly. This has resulted in the rise of AI-built applications. I witnessed the growth and development of AI capabilities and I think it would be interesting to see how the perception of builders has shifted from the previous months to this day.


### Methodology

1. **Data Collection**

   - Assign sentiment (positive/negative/neutral) per entry

4. **Analysis**
   - Aggregate sentiment by category and time period
   - Visualize trends numerically and graphically to observe how perception 
     has shifted over time
