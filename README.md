
# Data Engineering Pilipinas Cohort (2026-A)


## I'm Kinda Likin' the Vibe: Tracking Sentiment Shifts in a Filipino AI App-Building Community


### Overview

```
Since AI-assisted tools emerged, the barrier to building applications has dropped sharply, giving rise to AI-built applications. Having watched AI capabilities develop firsthand, I wanted to examine how builders' attitudes and output have shifted over recent months.
```

This project aims to understand how much the AI-assisted development in the Philippines has progressed through the previous months. By analyzing posts and comments from an AI app building community facebook group, [App Buiders PH](https://www.facebook.com/groups/980298767865372), we hope to extract the underlying trend and growth of AI-assisted application development.

This project analyzes posts and comments from [App Builders PH](https://www.facebook.com/groups/980298767865372), a Facebook community focused on AI app building, from its creation on May 05, 2026 until July 8, 2026. Using sentiment analysis, it aims to surface how builder attitudes toward AI-assisted development have shifted within the Philippine builder community over this period.

The project aims to answer: How have the attitudes of the App Builders PH community toward AI-assisted development shifted from the group's creation in May 2026 to July 2026, and what does that reveal about the maturing perception of AI-assisted building in the Philippines?


### Intended Audience

This project is targeted toward stakeholders interested in the state of AI-assisted development, particularly those trying to understand how the practice is being adopted and perceived in the Philippine context. It is especially relevant to developers who are still navigating and exploring how AI fits into their development workflows.
The primary audiences include:

* Software Developers - both those already using AI tools and those hesitant to adopt them, who want to understand how their peers in the local community are experiencing AI-assisted building.
* Aspiring Developers and Career Shifters - individuals drawn in by the lowered barrier to entry, who want a realistic sense of the community's sentiment before committing time and effort.
* Tech Educators and Bootcamp Instructors - those shaping curricula who need insight into how learners and practitioners actually feel about AI-assisted development.
* Startup Founders and Indie Builders - people building products with lean teams who rely on AI tooling and want to gauge community confidence and common pain points.
* AI Tool Developers and Vendors - companies building AI development tools who want authentic, unfiltered feedback on how their tools are received in an emerging market.
* Researchers and Analysts - those studying technology adoption, developer communities, or the socio-technical impact of AI in software development, especially within Southeast Asia.

### Project Objectives

1. **Data Collection**
   - Scrape posts and comments from the App Builder PH Facebook group using Apify from May 05, 2026 to July 8, 2026

2. **Data Preprocessing**
   - Prepare data for analysis

3. **Categorization & Sentiment Labeling**
   - Classify posts/comments into emerging thematic categories (e.g., trust/security, aesthetic taste, perceived competence)
   - Assign sentiment (positive/negative/neutral) per entry

4. **Analysis**
   - Aggregate sentiment by category and time period
   - Visualize trends numerically and graphically to observe how perception has shifted over time
